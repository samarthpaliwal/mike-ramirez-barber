Your appointment

What to expect

From the moment you sit down to the moment you check the mirror.

  1. Consultation

    You and Mike talk through what you want. Bring a photo if you have one. He'll tell you honestly what will work with your hair.

  2. The cut

    Clippers, shears and guards chosen for your hair type. Mike builds the shape first, then refines the blend.

  3. The finish

    Edges and line-up are sharpened, the neckline is cleaned up, and the beard is detailed if it's part of your service.

  4. Styling advice

    Mike styles the cut and shows you how to recreate it at home, including which product to use and how often to come back.
