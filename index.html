Meet Mike

A barber who treats every cut like his name is on it.

Mike Ramirez is a licensed barber with more than 15 years behind the chair. You'll find him at Double Edged Barbershop in Tucson.

Every appointment starts with a conversation, not the clippers. Mike asks how you wear your hair, what you liked about your last cut, and what you didn't, then builds the cut around your head shape, your hairline and your routine.

The approach is simple: steady hands, clean blends, crisp edges, and no rushing. Whether it's a first haircut for your kid or the same fade you've had for years, it gets the same attention.
